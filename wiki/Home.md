# WattWise Billing Wiki

Welcome to the team wiki of the **Billing** squad at WattWise Energy.

WattWise supplies electricity to household customers. Once a month, after the meter readings
for the 1st of the month come in, the **monthly billing run** creates an invoice for every
customer for the month before.
